# Glass Shards

{{ infobox("vanillaplus:glass_shards") }}

**Glass Shards** are items that can be dropped from [[mc:Glass]], [[mc:Glass Pane|Glass Panes]], [[mc:Stained Glass]] or [[mc:Stained Glass Pane|Stained Glass Panes]] if mined without a tool enchanted with Silk Touch.

## Usage

Shards can be used in the Crafting table to craft a Glass or Stained Glass Block.  
For that, 9 of the same Item are to be put into the Crafting Grid to obtain a single block.

## Obtaining

### Mining

Breaking Glass, Glass Panes, Stained Glass or Stained Glass Panes without a tool enchanted with Silk Touch has a 10% chance of dropping between 2 and 5 Shards for Glass and Stained Glass and between 1 and 3 for the Pane variants.
