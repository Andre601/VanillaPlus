# Cherry

{{ infobox("vanillaplus:cherry") }}

**Cherry** is a food item which can randomly drop from breaking [[mc:Cherry leaves]].

## Obtaining

### Breaking

Breaking Cherry Leaves has a 20% chance of dropping between 1 and 4 Cherries.
